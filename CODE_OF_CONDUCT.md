# Code of Conduct

This is a small hobby project. The expectation is simple: be respectful and
assume good faith.

- No harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language or behavior.
- Keep disagreements about code and design focused on the work, not the person.
- Respect that this is maintained on a volunteer basis — reviews and replies
  may take time.

Reports of unacceptable behavior can be made by opening an issue or
contacting the maintainer directly. Reports will be handled discreetly, and
violations may result in comments being removed or contributors being
blocked from the repository.
